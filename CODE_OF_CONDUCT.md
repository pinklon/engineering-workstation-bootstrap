# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, useful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that support an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- showing empathy and respect toward other people
- giving and accepting constructive technical feedback
- focusing criticism on ideas, code, and outcomes rather than people
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them
- respecting different experience levels and communication styles
- keeping discussions relevant to the project
- considering what is best for the community and the people using the software

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or discrimination
- sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal attacks
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission
- deliberate disruption of discussions, issues, reviews, or project infrastructure
- impersonation or deceptive representation
- any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

Technical disagreement is normal. Turning it into a referendum on another person's intelligence is not technical rigor; it is merely poor behavior wearing a keyboard.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, documentation, release channels, and other public or private interactions conducted on behalf of the project.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal information.

Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. People involved in enforcement must respect the privacy and security of reporters.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, reject, remove, or lock comments, commits, issues, pull requests, and other contributions that violate this code. They may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is harmful, repeated, or severe.

Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, intent, prior behavior, and the safety of the community.
